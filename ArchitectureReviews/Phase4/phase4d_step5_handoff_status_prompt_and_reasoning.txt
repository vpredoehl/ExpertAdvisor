REASONING

Phase 4D Step 4 completes the write-side handoff from an approved campaign into exact Phase 4C proposals. The safest next increment is not automatic review or execution. It is deterministic visibility into what happened after that handoff.

Without Step 5, an operator can inspect the materialization and inspect individual Phase 4C records, but there is no campaign-level answer to whether the whole approved set is awaiting review, partially reviewed, ready for execution, partially executed, ready for activation, or fully activated. That gap encourages ad-hoc SQL and makes inconsistencies harder to detect.

A read-only projection is the correct next boundary because it:

- preserves explicit human-controlled Phase 4C review, execution, and activation;
- adds no scheduler or experiment risk;
- treats the immutable materialization manifest as authoritative membership;
- exposes incomplete or contradictory downstream evidence rather than repairing it;
- provides the observability needed before considering any later campaign-level progression command;
- avoids persisting derived state that could become stale;
- exercises and validates the links between Phase 4D materialization and existing Phase 4C lifecycle records.

This step should be completed and reviewed before any campaign-level command is allowed to approve, execute, activate, or automatically advance materialized proposals.
