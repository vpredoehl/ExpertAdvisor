PHASE 6C INTENT

Phase 6C records one immutable administrative operator-review decision over one exact persisted Phase 6B follow-up proposal.

Supported decisions are exactly:

- `approved`
- `rejected`

An approved Phase 6C review means only:

- an operator administratively approved the exact persisted advisory proposal for possible consideration by a later explicitly authorized phase.

It does not mean:

- activated;
- execution authorized;
- follow-up authorized;
- queued;
- scheduled;
- scheduler work;
- scheduler signaled;
- worker launch;
- experiment creation;
- experiment modification;
- campaign success;
- profitability;
- statistical validation.

A rejected review must be equally immutable and auditable.

No Phase 6A or Phase 6B row may be mutated.
