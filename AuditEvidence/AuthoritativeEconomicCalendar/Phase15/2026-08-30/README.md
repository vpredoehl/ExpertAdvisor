The production-ready semantic contracts are limited to:

- GDP: BEA real GDP, advance/initial estimate only, annualized quarter-over-quarter scalar percent, `bea_real_gdp_annualized_quarterly_percent_v1`.
- Retail Sales: Census advance total retail and food services sales, month-over-month scalar percent, `census_advance_release_headline_mom_percent_v1`.

The persisted Employment consensus is a nonfarm-payroll count contract;
unemployment rate and average hourly earnings do not have separate compatible
event identities in this catalog and are not silently substituted. CPI has one
y/y consensus outlier among predominantly m/m rows, and PPI crosses a historical
finished-goods/final-demand measure regime; neither family receives a generic
percent parser.

GDP second/third/updated estimates are revision-only and excluded. Six Retail
artifacts do not prove the supported headline statistic under the fail-closed
parser and are classified `unsupported_semantics`.

No BLS release-specific artifacts are retained in `EconomicCalendar/raw/`.
The official BLS archive indexes expose release-specific historical material,
but direct repository acquisition attempts returned HTTP 403 on this host.
No current/revised BLS table was substituted for missing release evidence.

FOMC statement artifacts are retained, but narrative decisions are not
manufactured into actuals. Most selected FOMC consensus rows are ranges, while
the runtime authoritative surprise channels require scalar forecast/actual
pairs, so no FOMC payload is produced.
